Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?
Find the correct path and move the football pieces to solve each puzzle. Plan your moves carefully and complete the level.

The game is not responding correctly.
Close and reopen the app. If the problem continues, please contact support.

Will my progress be saved?
Game progress may be stored locally on your device. Removing the app may also remove locally stored progress.
