Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Operator and scope

ToolFork is operated by Samet Sevim / TWorld Software. This policy covers the ToolFork informational website and the local ToolFork Studio social publishing workflow.

2. Information handled by the application

  • Connected account identifiers, usernames and profile information returned by authorized platform APIs.
  • Access and refresh tokens, granted permissions and token expiration information when available.
  • Selected videos, captions, accessibility text and publishing preferences.
  • Upload identifiers, post links, processing status and technical error information.

TikTok authorization takes place through TikTok. ToolFork does not request or store your TikTok account password.

3. Why this information is used

This information is used to connect and verify the intended account, display available publishing options, transfer selected content, check processing status, refresh authorized sessions and help prevent duplicate posts.

4. Storage

The current workflow runs locally. Configuration, content and publishing records are stored on the computer running ToolFork. OAuth tokens managed by the application are stored in its encrypted local token store. Other configured credentials may be held in local environment files and must be protected by the installation owner.

5. Transfers to other services

When an account is connected or a transfer is requested, the information needed for that action is sent to the relevant platform, such as TikTok. That platform processes the information under its own privacy policy.

If the Telegram control interface is used, commands, status messages and any content sent through that interface are also processed by Telegram.

ToolFork does not sell personal information or use connected account data for advertising profiling.

6. This website

These informational pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising trackers or application login forms. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

GitHub Privacy Statement

7. Retention and deletion

Local credentials and records remain until removed by the installation owner or the application's cleanup processes. You can revoke platform access in the connected account's settings and remove locally stored credentials, videos and records.

Revoking access or deleting local files does not automatically delete content already transferred to a social platform. Manage that content directly on the destination platform.

For questions or deletion requests concerning information held by the operator, contact the email below. Do not send passwords or access tokens by email.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may change as ToolFork develops. Updates will be published on this page.
