ToolFork

A personal tool for preparing short videos and managing uploads to the owner's connected social media accounts.

How it works

  1. Prepare and preview a video in the local ToolFork Studio.
  2. Connect a supported social account through its authorization flow.
  3. Select a video and review the destination account and publishing options.
  4. Confirm the transfer and check its processing status.

TikTok integration

The TikTok integration is under development and testing. It is designed to authorize the owner's account and transfer selected videos using TikTok's Content Posting API.

Available publishing options depend on TikTok permissions, account settings and application approval. When using the inbox upload option, the owner completes publication in TikTok.

Availability

ToolFork Studio runs locally and is currently intended for the owner's personal workflow. Public registration and downloads are not currently offered. This website provides product and policy information; it does not upload videos.
