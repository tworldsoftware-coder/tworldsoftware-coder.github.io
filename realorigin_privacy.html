RealOrigin — Privacy Policy & Support

RealOrigin helps you assess whether an image is likely to be authentic or AI-generated.

Privacy by design: image analysis is performed directly on your device. Images analyzed by RealOrigin are not uploaded to our servers for AI processing.

Privacy

RealOrigin is designed to minimize the collection and transmission of user data.

Images selected, shared, or analyzed using RealOrigin are processed locally on your device using on-device technology. RealOrigin does not require these images to be uploaded to a remote server in order to perform image authenticity analysis.

RealOrigin does not require you to create an account to perform standard image analysis.

Images and Photos

Images may be provided to RealOrigin by selecting them within the app or by using supported iOS sharing features such as the Share Sheet.

Images are used only for the purpose of performing the requested analysis and presenting the result to you.

RealOrigin does not sell, rent, or use your analyzed images for advertising purposes.

On-Device Analysis

RealOrigin performs its image analysis directly on compatible Apple devices. This means that an internet connection is not required for standard image analysis.

Analysis results are probabilistic and should be treated as an indication rather than a forensic determination. No automated AI-image detection system can guarantee 100% accuracy for every image.

Local Data

RealOrigin may store certain information locally on your device, such as app preferences, settings, analysis history, or cached analysis results where required for app functionality.

Locally stored data may be removed when the app is deleted or its data is cleared.

Advertising and Support

RealOrigin may display advertising or offer optional ways to support the continued development of the app.

If third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or similar services are added, this policy will be updated to identify those services and explain the categories of information they may process.

Third-Party Services

RealOrigin's core image analysis does not depend on a remote AI service.

Certain optional app functionality may rely on Apple platform services or other third-party technologies. Those services operate according to their own privacy policies and applicable platform requirements.

Children's Privacy

RealOrigin does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been submitted through our support channels, please contact us so that we can review and remove it where appropriate.
